# Island identity and original vector stickers

`island-mark.svg` is the shared island/sun/palm/wave site mark. It replaces the previous bird-character mark in navigation and is also the favicon. The bird courier remains part of the post office, not the site logo.

`island-stickers.svg` contains fourteen original SVG symbols. The first six are `jellyfish`, `shell`, `bottle`, `orbit`, `cloud`, and `postcard`; sea2 adds `sunset`, `wave`, `lantern`, `starfish`, `moon-sea`, `spiral-sea`, `tape`, and `sprig`. They reference the user's boards only for pastel blue/pink/lilac palette, sea motifs, white sticker edges and soft highlights. No supplied-image pixels, watermarks, app logos, artist signatures or sample-sheet labels are copied or removed. These symbols are decorative objects, not new islander OCs or official card artwork.

The original two heading accents are retained. Larger four-piece sea keepsake groups continue between page sections, with different combinations and placements: sunset/wave/starfish on the home shore, lantern/starfish/sprig by the office, moonlit sea/lantern near the postal collection, moon/shell by the album and jellyfish/shell/wave beside the gallery. They are not new feature cards or interactive controls; previously removed homepage entry/story panels remain removed. Mobile groups are smaller and contained inside the page width.

Decorations are marked `aria-hidden`, ignore pointer events and remain static. No extra animation loop, remote image request or library is introduced. Mobile placement keeps title text and controls readable; the glass/water backgrounds remain the existing ones.
